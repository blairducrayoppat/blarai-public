# Disposition — coder-fleet integrity arc, four independent reviews (2026-07-30 pm)

Four branches across two repos went to independent author≠verifier reviewers. **Three came back
BLOCK**, and every branch was amended at least once by someone who had not written it. This record
disposes every finding from all four reviews plus their fix-verification rounds.

The arc's unifying defect: an instrument that reports confidently while measuring nothing. Each of
the four new controls carried a version of the defect it was built to remove — `assert True`
minting `GREEN-VERIFIED`, a rulebook graded as source by the scanner it configures, a pinned
worktree whose detached HEAD made a correct night look like a failed one, and a build-output tier
that un-graded Node's authored entry point.

Cross-repo evidence uses the `<repo>@<sha>` form landed by #1090 (`eda71a0f`); bare SHAs are BlarAI.

## Carried, not dispositioned

The reviewers' verified-sound lists stand as independent corroboration and assert no defect: the
absence of any fallback path in the pinning work, the completeness of the CODE/STATE split, the
144-repo false-positive measurement, the mutation-testing evidence that every paired test has
teeth, and the traceability advisory contract. Execution evidence is the branch verifiers
(driver-pinning 36/0, scorecard-scan 42/0, native-error-scoping 29/0, pass-banking-freeze 12/0,
task-settings 8/0 against the live task) plus the pytest suites (security 141, traceability 50,
runtime-root 20, disposition 39), and the merged-main standing gate re-measured at merge time.

**One finding is deliberately absent from the block above because it is not a defect in any
branch:** three separate actors — the traceability builder, its reviewer, and the session
integrating them — independently made the same `tail`-truncation error reading a 223-line CLI
output through a 25-line window, and each reported a corpus conclusion drawn from ~11% of the
result. No branch caused it; the tool invited it. The structural control ships with #1184's CLI
change (aggregate printed last, with a self-checking denominator) and the lesson is recorded in
the journal fragment at `60eb92cb`.
