## What the reviewer attacked and could not break

- **The four identity layers in `governed_core` are real and correct** — inode identity, link-count
  refusal, worktree pointer resolution, content sentinel. Verified to deny a renamed clone. The
  card's description of the *module* was accurate; only its placement was wrong.
- **`verify(full=True)` has no production caller**; **verify-on-load is wired in both production
  constructors and fails closed**; **ADR-029's 7–13 ms and the 1,000-record production clamp both
  check out** against the banked measurement.
- **Claim-register row 28 says what the card says it says** — no off-by-one.
- **The vsock admission is honest** — nothing worse is hiding behind the Open row.
- **Image generation is not a missing row** (it rides the tool loop); voice has no direct action
  surface; the public-mirror publisher is dev-side and outside the runtime this card scopes.
