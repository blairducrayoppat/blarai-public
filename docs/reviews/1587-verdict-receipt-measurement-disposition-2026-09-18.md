## The honest note

The ticket was opened because someone measured a component and reported it as the whole. I measured
the whole and reported it without the variable it depends on — and then explained it with a mechanism
I had not profiled. Both errors have the same shape as the one being corrected: a number that is true
about *something* presented as true about the system.

The part that held up is the part that was counted rather than computed. The verifier did not infer
per-record chip work from the magnitude of the timings; it wrapped the signer and counted the calls.
Every figure in this ticket that survived contact with an adversary was one where somebody counted
something.
