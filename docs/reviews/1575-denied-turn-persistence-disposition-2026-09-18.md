## What the reviewer attacked and could not break

- **The control has teeth.** Forcing the rewrite to fire unconditionally is caught by
  `test_AN_UNREFUSED_TURN_STILL_PERSISTS_THE_REAL_ANSWER`. This was the top suspicion named in the
  review brief and it did not hold.
- **No stale-notice-over-a-real-answer path exists.** All seven notice assignments are immediately
  followed by an unconditional break, including a specific check of the `propose_preference`
  `continue`.
- **All four originally driven paths genuinely leak before the fix and genuinely stop after it.**
- **The exception-text suppression works** — the raised exception's text does not reach the
  transcript.

## The honest note

The scan this change shipped was the same construct that had been defeated the day before, in the
same session, on a different ticket — and its own docstring already said source scanning is not
evidence anything runs. Writing that sentence did not stop me relying on it for three paths whose
only alternative was ten lines of test each.
