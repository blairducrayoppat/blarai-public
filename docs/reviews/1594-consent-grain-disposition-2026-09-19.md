## The honest note

Six of seven findings are documentation, which is easy to read as "nothing serious". Two of them are
the surfaces this project designates as the authority a session must read instead of trusting its own
memory — and one was made wrong by the very commit that changed the posture, through a line citation
that was accurate the moment before. A register that cites line numbers decays every time somebody
adds a comment above the thing cited, and the register is the document the operator is told to trust.
