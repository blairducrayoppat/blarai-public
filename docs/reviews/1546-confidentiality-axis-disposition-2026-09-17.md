## What the reviewer attacked and could not break

- **The five integrity tiers** are exactly as the note names them, confirmed by execution.
- **The lattice math** — most-restrictive-wins as a composition rule, and a total order rather than
  incomparable elements — is sound. The reviewer tried to construct a BlarAI content class needing
  incomparability and could not. The objection is to the *unit* and the *duplicate vocabulary*, not
  the algebra.
- **The demand for a measured refusal rate before enforcement** is correct and well-argued — the
  reviewer called it the best paragraph in the note.
- **No contradiction** with the #1544 Auditability Card, the #1570 disclosure, or claim-register
  rows 21–22.
- **No audit-chain impact**: AO provenance is not persisted, hashed or serialised.
