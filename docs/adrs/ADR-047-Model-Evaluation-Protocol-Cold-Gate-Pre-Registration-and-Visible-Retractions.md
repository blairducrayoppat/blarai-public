## Context

The first measurements of the Qwen3.5 small models produced clean, plausible, wrong numbers three separate ways before any comparison was reported: the machine heat-throttled, a perturbation test changed nothing for one model, and a label typo silently dropped every small-versus-large comparison (journal fragment `2026-10-04_qwen35-4b-phase1-instrument-before-comparison.md`). The arc then needed several retractions in place (listed in Decision 6). This ADR records the protocol that came out of it so the next arc starts from it instead of rediscovering it.

The physical fact the protocol is built around, measured on this machine (#1663 comment 3843): sustained back-to-back decode THROTTLES. The same prompt on the 4B without MTP went from 48 ms to about 160 ms per token over about 30 generations (about 2 minutes), with the thermal zone climbing from 77 to 96 C; a fresh process started hot also started slow; a rest of 20 to 60 s brought it back to about 48 ms in 4 of 5 probes (one 40 s rest read 67 ms). Below that heat-soak, decode was steady (4B MTP-off 20.2 to 20.9 tok/s over three visits, CV about 1%). A faster arm finishes sooner and so heats less than a slower one, so interleaving alone does not remove the confound.

## Decision

1. **COLD protocol is primary: the thermal zone must be at or below 72 C before each generation.** Thermal state is stamped on every row. A PLATEAU protocol (heat loop to stable time per token, then repeated reps) is recorded but not used for ratios, because the heat-soak confound survives it (plateau CV 10 to 14% on some 4B cells). The two are never mixed.
2. **The unit of analysis is the visit mean:** one load of one arm. Arms are interleaved across invocations with an ABA bracket, one load at a time, and a model that is not under test is never co-resident. A repeated arm is repeated as a visit, not as back-to-back generations. Phase 1 ran 8 invocations (4 to 5 visits per arm); the 9B top-up brought cells to 9 to 10 visits.
3. **Limits are pre-registered before the run, on the ticket.** Declared in #1663 comment 3843 and again for the 9B in #1665 comment 3851: a decode difference under about 5% is not a finding; a first-token-delay difference under about 30% is not a finding; any ratio whose arm CV exceeds 15% is REFUSED (reported as unformable, with the reason). The answer to a refusal is more visits or a longer cold gate, not a stop and not a looser limit. The 9B arm also declared a prediction that could be wrong ("the 9B passes `pa-mdl-007` and `pa-mdl-013`"); it was wrong. Instrument variance is measured before comparing (the Phase 1 pilot was 3 visits in an ABA bracket, CV about 1%; the 9B variance probe was 12 Policy Agent and 3 multi-turn generations per arm, cold-gated). The pre-registration itself fixed the CV rule and the visit mean as the unit; the visit counts (4 to 5 per arm, 9 to 10 after the 9B top-up) came from the campaign design, not from a stated minimum n.
4. **Rows that never reached the gate are excluded by protocol definition, and the exclusion is disclosed as post-hoc.** In the 9B top-up, 5 of 159 cold rows had `cold_wait.reached == False` (waited about 182 s, thermal zone still 77 to 84 C), all in one invocation, run while review and builder agents were also working on the box (a hypothesis for the heat; it was not shown to be the cause, as the verdict says). The pre-registered analyser had pooled them silently (`invalid_rows` 0). The rule was added after the data was read and applies uniformly on the gate flag, not on the outcome; the pooled numbers are kept (`speed_summary_with_gate_failures.json`) and the excluded rows listed under `cold_gate_not_reached`. It still leaves a weakness the verdict states: the flag is the wait loop's last poll, not the temperature read before each generation, and 38 of the 154 kept 9B cold rows (24.7%) started at 72.9 to 81.9 C (4B: 36 of 69). The strict-start sensitivity (`--strict-start-temp 72`) moves 9B-MTP-on long-prompt visits from 9 to 5 and its MTP on-over-off ratio from 2.17 to 2.30, and leaves the headline 9B-over-14B ratios unchanged (1.715, 1.164, 1.076 to 1.718, 1.164, 1.077; reproduced exactly by the round-3 verifier).
5. **Exploratory results are never findings.** A median view added after the CV rule refused several ratios is labelled exploratory, not pre-registered, and not used for any claim. **Superseded figures, kept visible:** before the gate exclusion the 9B-MTP-on long-prompt cell had a CV of 26.4% and the 14B on the newer runtime 23.7% (#1665 comment 3855), and those ratios were refused. **After the exclusion of the 5 not-gated rows (Decision 4) the 9B long-prompt cell is at 14.4% (#1665 comment 3868) and the 14B on 2026.4 at 14.1%, just inside the 15% limit, so those ratios are now stated, with the verdict calling them less firm.** The post-hoc exclusion is what moved them from refused to stated, which is why Decision 4 discloses it and the strict-start sensitivity.
6. **Retractions are made in place, where the claim was made, and the wrong version stays visible** (struck through or quoted above the correction). The record of this arc:
   - "MTP on and off outputs byte-identical in every check / 63 of 63" (Phase 1 verdict, journal, log, README): the comparison examined one run. Recomputed over every pinned run: 9B 2 of 366 calls differ, 4B 1 of 377, mid-reply; per-case pass or fail agrees in every run (`fad4c32b` and the 9B arc).
   - "The 4B lets two dangerous actions through and I do not know why", and the candidate mechanism "no metadata or audit-log example in the deny list": the command is never shown to any model (ADR-044); the 14B's DENY is on the rejection marker.
   - The session's own recommendation of a deterministic deny on schema rejection (#1673 comment 3864, retracted 3865 and 3866; ADR-044).
   - "The slow visit overlapped review-agent CPU work (23% sampler CPU)": that was one 7-second sample; the 120 s window mean was 16.8% and another visit at 24% CPU decoded normally. The cause shown by the data was rows that never reached the gate (#1665 comment 3856).
   - "Byte-identical Policy Agent outputs across 3 runs" (the comparison hashed the parsed label and confidence, not the raw text): reworded; raw text was compared for one run only.
   - The Jev ideation probability figures (rows counted harmless controls as wrong ALLOWs); corrected twice to 6 informative rows, probability 0.3999 to 0.7119, all below 0.75 (#1672 comment 3867, #1665 comment 3869).
   - The public Hugging Face cards carried the retracted byte-identity claims until corrected cards were uploaded at ship.
7. **Independent review that RUNS the code; the author is never the verifier.** Reviewers on Opus read and ran the code and wrote no fixes; builders and other subagents on Sonnet (LA direction, #1665 comment 3850). Phase 1: reviewer findings F1 to F15 and 31 regression locks (4 mutation-checked), disposition 41 of 41. The 9B arc: round 1 (2 blocking, 9 should-fix, 8 notes), round 2 (2 blocking, 6 should-fix, 6 notes), round 3 (verification; one blocking, the missing disposition record, and 4 should-fix, 3 notes), disposition 40 of 40. Findings the author did not find: the 14B runtime label mismatch that silently skipped the three comparisons the ticket exists for; a V1 perturbation that changed 0 of 23 prompts for the 4B (its template trims message bodies) while the analysis printed "no verdict flipped"; the publisher accepting an option-shaped path argument (could delete the public dataset); MTP on versus off not byte-identical.
8. **Raw data, analyses and conditions are published to the Hugging Face dataset `blairducrayoppat/openvino-arc140v-lunarlake`** in the same arc as the `PERFORMANCE_LOG.md` entry; an arc without the upload is an incomplete ship (LA requirement, 2026-10-04, applies to all future research measurements). Existing dataset extended where it fits: config `qwen35_small` and its sibling configs (`_speed_rows`, `_multiturn_ttft`, `_quality_runs`, `_quality_cases`), with 4B and 9B folders. Commit ids as recorded on the tickets (not re-verified for this ADR): 4B data `5d70f49774acc63f7379ef95141226efe7436d17`, card `1b06439fb7d54c1c0f4f19eda19e9a0360e885cc`; 9B folder and root card `1e52be8a`, `68b2bc47`, 4B folder rebuilt `0f7aa973`. No generated text and no local username in the data. Community-grade stamp on every record: hardware, driver, runtime builds, model and precision, exact commands, mean and spread with n, conditions (what else was running), and an explicit NOT-measured list.
9. **Quality is judged on suites with repeats, not single runs.** Policy Agent classification x3 per arm (random marker), agentic and conversation x3 random and x3 pinned plus the V1 to V3 perturbations; answer quality and preference memory n=1 per arm, and no conclusion drawn from one run; a Policy Agent difference is called only when it repeats 3 of 3. The MTP engagement witness must pass or the arm's MTP numbers are void.

## The model-swap position (as of this record)

**Qwen3-14B stays** as the shared brain (ADR-012; CLAUDE.md `stack`). Replacing it is the LA's decision and go-live ceremony, and no arc here made it. What the evidence says, with conditions: Arc 140V, OpenVINO and GenAI 2026.4.0 for the Qwen3.5 arms, 2026.2.1 for the 14B as it ships, GPU driver 32.0.101.8831, AC power and High-performance plan, Bitdefender running, COLD protocol.

## Alternatives considered

- **Fixed cool-down between arms.** Rejected: the faster arm finishes sooner and heats less, so even a perfect interleave of the plateau regime favours it.
- **Plateau protocol as primary.** Recorded, not used for ratios, for the same reason.
- **Keep the 5 not-gated rows, as pre-registered** (the pooled rule refused 10 speed ratios): kept beside the exclusion as a disclosed alternative; the protocol definition ("COLD means gated") decided it.
- **Loosen the CV limit to form the refused ratios.** Rejected: a limit moved after the data is not a limit.
- **Attribute the slow visit to concurrent CPU work.** Retracted, see 6.

## Consequences

- Every future model or hardware arc declares its limits on the ticket before it runs and starts from the committed harness: `scripts/run_evals_instrumented.py`, `scripts/watch_eval_run.py`, `scripts/sample_system.py`, `scripts/probe_speed_variance.py`, `evals/cb_pipeline.py` engine arms.
- #1664 (coder offload ratios 20, 30, 40; Bitdefender exception on and off; Unified Telemetry n=3 per ratio) is to run under this protocol; it has not run.
- Review and builder agents do not run on the box during a thermally gated measurement (concurrent agent work was present when 5 rows missed the gate; causation was not shown, see 4).
